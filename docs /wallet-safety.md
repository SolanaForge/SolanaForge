# Wallet Safety Guide

## SolanaForge Wallet Security Principles

SolanaForge is a non-custodial Solana platform focused on transparent and user-controlled SPL token creation.

Users always maintain full control of their wallets and digital assets.

SolanaForge does not store, access, or control user funds.

---

## Never Share Your Seed Phrase

SolanaForge will NEVER ask for:
- seed phrases
- private keys
- wallet recovery phrases

If any website or individual requests this information, do not provide it.

---

## Transparent Wallet Interactions

All wallet interactions on SolanaForge are:
- user-initiated
- visible to the user
- confirmed directly inside the wallet

No hidden approvals or background signatures are performed.

---

## Transaction Simulation

Before transactions are signed, SolanaForge may display:
- estimated Solana network fees
- metadata costs
- account rent costs
- platform service fees
- revoke authority settings

Simulation does not execute blockchain transactions.

No wallet signatures occur during simulation.

---

## Verify Transactions Carefully

Before signing any transaction:
- review wallet prompts carefully
- verify token settings
- confirm revoke selections
- understand estimated fees

Always verify the destination and purpose of transactions before approval.

---

## Platform Transparency

SolanaForge aims to provide:
- transparent fee visibility
- clear transaction previews
- understandable wallet prompts
- educational onboarding for beginners

All blockchain actions require explicit wallet approval from the user.

---

## Supported Wallets

Examples include:
- Phantom
- Solflare
- other compatible Solana wallets
